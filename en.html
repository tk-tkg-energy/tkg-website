03 / SCOPE

What we do not do.

We would rather say this at the start than halfway through a project.

  • Permit filingsFiled by the owner or a licensed agent. We prepare the technical content and the roadmap.
  • Statutory safety rolesWe do not take them on or lend our name to them.
  • Certification or lender's TA reportsOur reviews are for your internal decisions.
  • Import, distribution or salesNo warranty or after-sales obligations.
  • Electrical and grid designDone by specialists; we review it from the owner's side.
  • Full-time or resident workRemote by default, on site when it matters.
