02 / SERVICES

Services.

Scoped to the decision in front of you. A single review is fine.

01 / REGULATORY SCREENING

Japanese regulatory screening

  • Which Japanese laws and codes apply to the plant, such as the High Pressure Gas Safety Act and the Gas Business Act
  • What they require of the design, and where permits and inspections are likely to set the schedule
  • Written report in English
02 / DOCUMENT REVIEW

Design and quotation review

  • Comments on drawings, calculations, specifications and quotation breakdowns from Japanese vendors and EPC contractors
  • Where the design margin goes beyond what the codes require, we point it out and propose value engineering that keeps the safety basis intact
03 / EPC PMO

EPC project management support

  • Weekly meetings to track design, procurement and schedule issues
  • Support in technical negotiations with vendors, in English and Japanese
04 / INSPECTION

Inspection and commissioning

  • Review of inspection procedures and attendance at factory and site inspections
  • Commissioning planning and follow-up of open items
03 / SCOPE

What we do not do.

We would rather say this at the start than halfway through a project.

  • File permit applications on your behalf.Filings are made by the project owner or a licensed agent. We prepare the technical content and the roadmap.
  • Take on statutory safety roles, or lend our name to them.
  • Issue certifications, conformity inspections or lender's technical advisor reports.Our reviews are for your internal decisions.
  • Act as importer, distributor or sales agent, or carry warranty and after-sales obligations.
  • Design electrical systems or grid connections.These are outside our field. Where a project needs them, the work goes to specialists and we review it from the owner's side.
  • Work full-time or stay resident on site.
